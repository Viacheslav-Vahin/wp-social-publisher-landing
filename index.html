FAQ

Does this use external SaaS servers?

No. The plugin runs on your WordPress site. Data and tokens stay in your own environment.

Can I publish to multiple accounts at once?

Yes. One post can be sent to multiple selected accounts in a single action.

Does Send now require a schedule?

No. Send now posts immediately to selected accounts with no schedule required.

Which post types are supported?

v1 supports WordPress Posts and Pages.
